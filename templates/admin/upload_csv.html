FILE FORMAT

Prepare your CSV

Include these four columns in your first row, in any order.

Student name Year Course Amount
Example CSV 
STUDENT NAME,YEAR,COURSE,AMOUNT
JOSHUA ZAMORA ABUNALES,2,BSIS,500.00
CRISHA MAE ABOGADA ABIADO,1,BSCS,250.00

Student name, year, and course must match exactly one student.

Amounts must be positive numbers, such as 250.00.

Unmatched or ambiguous rows are skipped and reported after import.
